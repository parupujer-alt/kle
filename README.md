# BCA College Gokak - Official College Portal

A modern, responsive, and feature-rich institutional website developed for **BCA College Gokak** (Gokak Institute of Computer Applications & Technology), affiliated to **Rani Channamma University (RCU), Belagavi** and recognized by the Government of Karnataka.

Engineered with a **Vibrant Orange and Crisp White** visual identity.

---

## 🌟 Key Features & Sections

### 1. **Vibrant Orange & White Design System**
- High-contrast, elegant palette: Tangerine/Saffron Orange (`#ea580c`, `#f97316`), Warm Cream/Amber tints (`#fff7ed`, `#ffedd5`), and Pure White card surfaces (`#ffffff`).
- Fully responsive on smartphones, tablets, and desktop displays.

### 2. **Rich Imagery & High-Definition Photo Gallery**
- Real photos showcasing modern computer laboratories, academic buildings, campus grounds, tech hackathons, coding workshops, cultural fests, and annual sports meets.
- **Interactive Fullscreen Lightbox**: Click any gallery photo to open in a high-resolution modal with detailed captions.
- Category filters: *All Photos, Computer Labs, Campus & Library, Hackathons & Events, Placements, Sports Day*.

### 3. **NEP-2020 Semester-Wise Curriculum Browser**
- Interactive tabbed syllabus browser from **Semester 1 to Semester 6**.
- Detailed theory vs. practical lab subject breakdown.
- 1-click download button for official RCU Belagavi syllabus.

### 4. **Campus Facilities & Infrastructure**
- **Hi-Tech Computer Laboratories**: 120+ Intel Core i7 workstations, dual OS (Linux/Windows), 300 Mbps fiber LAN, UPS backup.
- **Digital Library & E-Learning**: Over 8,500 books, IEEE e-journals, DELNET access, and NPTEL course materials.
- **Smart Multimedia Classrooms**: Interactive smart boards, laser projectors, and acoustic audio systems.
- **Auditorium**: 350-seater central hall for technical symposiums and coding battles.
- **College Bus Fleet**: Daily safe transit covering Gokak, Ghataprabha, Konnur, Ankalagi, and surrounding taluk villages.

### 5. **Placement Cell & Recruiter Showcase**
- Placement highlights: **100% Placement Assistance, 8.4 LPA Highest CTC, 4.2 LPA Average CTC**.
- Corporate recruiter badges: *TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Cognizant, Tech Mahindra, Capgemini, Mindtree, HCLTech*.
- Verified alumni testimonials from graduates working in Bangalore, Pune, and Hyderabad.

### 6. **Faculty Directory**
- Dedicated profiles of BCA professors and lecturers detailing their academic credentials (M.C.A., M.Tech, Ph.D.), teaching experience, and technical specializations.

### 7. **Online Admission Application & Merit Estimator**
- Interactive registration form with instant percentage calculation.
- Auto-generates official **Application Registration Number** (e.g. `BCA-GK-2026-4829`) and verification pass.
- Scholarship information: Government SSP/NSP scholarship guidance and college merit fee waivers.

### 8. **Live Notice Board & Campus Contact Info**
- Official RCU exam timetables, internal test schedules, and scholarship circulars.
- Detailed campus address on Falls Road near Court Circle, Gokak, admissions helpline phone numbers, and Google Maps location.
